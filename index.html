Campaign Platform

Fitch for the Future

Fitch for the Future is about building a stronger, more affordable 1st Berkshire District where opportunity reaches every town and every family. The campaign focuses on revitalizing downtowns, expanding transportation and broadband, and investing in education and workforce pathways that keep talent here and attract new opportunity. By tackling housing affordability, supporting seniors and families, and championing universal childcare, this vision keeps our communities livable now and for generations to come. Above all, it’s a commitment to hands-on, collaborative leadership that ensures the District is  heard, included, and positioned to thrive.
